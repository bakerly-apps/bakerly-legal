Privacy Policy

App: Einbürgerungstest · Publisher: Bakerly Apps · Last updated: 27 May 2026 · Version 2

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app Einbürgerungstest (“the app”), published by Bakerly Apps, handles information. The app is an independent practice application and is not affiliated with the German Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF), the German government, or any other official authority.

Summary

  • No account is required.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Your learning progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and is financed by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob collects limited data (see “Advertising” below) to display and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings and study data locally on your phone or tablet using the app’s private storage (for example: app language, federal state selection, quiz answers, bookmarks, spaced-repetition schedules, and practice-test history).

We do not operate servers that receive this study data. It is not uploaded to Bakerly Apps.

You can remove this data by:

  • Using in-app options such as resetting your progress, or
  • Clearing the app’s storage in your device’s Android settings, or
  • Uninstalling the app (which removes local app data from your device).

Information we do not collect

Bakerly Apps does not collect from you through the app:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or files outside the app
  • Payment or financial information (the app is free and has no in-app purchases)

If you contact us by email, we receive only what you choose to send (for example your email address and message content) so we can reply.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner and rectangular ads inside the app. AdMob is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google acts as an independent data controller for the advertising data it processes.

When ads are shown, Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier assigned by Android)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not GPS)
  • Device information such as device model, operating system, browser type, language, and time zone
  • Interaction with ads (impressions, clicks)

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and — where you have consented — to personalize ads. Full details are in Google’s policies:

  • How Google uses data when you use its partners’ sites or apps
  • Google Privacy Policy

EU/EEA users — GDPR consent for personalized ads

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, when you first open the app a consent dialog provided by Google (User Messaging Platform / UMP) will ask whether Google may use your data to personalize ads.

  • If you consent, Google may use the data above to show ads tailored to your interests.
  • If you decline or have not consented, you will see non-personalized ads: ads based on the current context only, not on profiling.
  • You can change this choice at any time from the consent settings in the app, or by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings to be asked again.

How to limit ad tracking on Android

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” (on Android 12 and newer) or “Opt out of Ads Personalization” (on older versions). After this, AdMob will no longer use a stable advertising ID to profile you.

Legal basis under the GDPR

  • Provision of the app and offline study progress (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — necessary to provide the service you requested.
  • Non-personalized ads and security/fraud prevention by Google (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — Google’s and our legitimate interest in financing and protecting a free app.
  • Personalized advertising (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — only with your explicit consent through the Google consent dialog.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for the German citizenship test. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads served in the app are not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data that we process about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Germany: the data-protection authority of your federal state, or the Bundesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit). To exercise rights against Bakerly Apps, contact us at the email below.

For data processed by Google through AdMob, please contact Google directly using the links in the “Advertising” section above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date and version number at the top will change when we do. The app may notify you in-app when the policy version changes so you can review the update before continuing.
