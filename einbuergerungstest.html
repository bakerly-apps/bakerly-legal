Privacy Policy

App: Einbürgerungstest · Publisher: Bakerly Apps · Last updated: 27 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile app Einbürgerungstest (“the app”), published by Bakerly Apps, handles information. The app is an independent practice application and is not affiliated with the German Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF), the German government, or any other official authority.

Summary

  • No account is required.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs in this app.
  • Your learning progress is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores settings and study data locally on your phone or tablet using the app’s private storage (for example: app language, federal state selection, quiz answers, bookmarks, spaced-repetition schedules, and practice-test history).

We do not operate servers that receive this study data. It is not uploaded to Bakerly Apps.

You can remove this data by:

  • Using in-app options such as resetting your progress, or
  • Clearing the app’s storage in your device’s Android settings, or
  • Uninstalling the app (which removes local app data from your device).

Information we do not collect

Through the app itself, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or files outside the app
  • Payment or financial information (the app is free and has no in-app purchases)

If you contact us by email, we receive only what you choose to send (for example your email address and message content) so we can reply.

Internet access

The app is designed to work offline. The Android version may declare general network access for technical reasons related to the app framework; the app does not transmit your study data to us over the internet.

If you open an external link from the app (for example the official BAMF question catalog PDF), that website is operated by a third party and has its own privacy practices.

Children

The app is intended for people preparing for the German citizenship test. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
